Why tint your windows

What the right tint actually does for your car.

Good tint is about more than a darker look. It changes how your car feels the second the Florida sun hits the glass — cooler, calmer, and easier on your eyes and your interior.

Beat the heat

Quality film turns back a real share of the sun's heat, so your cabin cools faster and your AC stops fighting a greenhouse every time you park in the sun.

Cut the glare

Knock down blinding glare from the sun and oncoming headlights. Easier drives, less eye strain, and a clearer view of the road day or night.

Protect your interior

The same rays that heat the cabin fade and crack your dash, seats and trim. Film blocks a heavy dose of UV so your interior keeps its color and value.

Privacy & a clean look

Keep prying eyes off your belongings and give your ride a sharp, factory-match finish — tint shaded and placed to suit your car and Florida's rules.
